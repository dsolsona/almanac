## Outcome

compiled union coverage and maintained partial-hour populations, consumed through
the shared exact totals evaluator. It names bounded raw-page preparation,
source-hour fences, old-snapshot recovery, exact compatibility proofs and the
fragmentation/unchanged-overlap cases that must pass before adoption. It does not
turn the dirty-interior fallback experiment into an accepted production path.

The primary prepared this while independent agents reviewed retention and
verified its populated forward upgrade. The public activation/default proposal
remains a separate pending maintainer decision.
