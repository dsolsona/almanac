## Outcome and scope

Establish real accuracy, page latency, live-collection isolation, retention and
recovery evidence for larger installations. The original 100- and 1,000-SLO
capacity targets remain unverified. Ten-SLO results cannot substitute for them.
The maintainer explicitly requested finishing ten SLOs now and deferring larger
tests; no larger workload is authorized as part of the current phase.

## Resume conditions and acceptance

Agree a bounded workload, machine/storage budget and acceptable setup cost before
execution. Reuse validated retained data rather than repeating year imports after
fixture-only corrections. Record independent and shared sources, retained-history
coverage, normal and added-100-ms network cohorts, real rendered journeys, exact
numerical expectations and connected VictoriaTraces. Keep SPEC-0019 latency,
freshness, accuracy, security and expiry limits unchanged.

The current approved performance entry is intentionally restricted to ten SLOs.
A later larger-fleet entry needs a concrete bounded scope and its actual command
authorization before this task becomes ready. Do not enable it during T08 final
verification. Preserve all earlier failed evidence and timing records.
