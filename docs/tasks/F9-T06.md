### Limits and remaining evidence

- The suite's readiness condition proves current budgets and list history
  are published. It does not assert that every historical point/tile over
  35 days is fully materialized or that worker queues are empty. These bytes
  describe the existing perf journey's snapshot, not a fully populated
  SLO-year; they cannot validate the review's per-year estimates by scaling.
- Both one-minute samples remain unmeasured after the collector failure.
  Background statements per minute per SLO are also unavailable:
  `pg_stat_statements_loaded=false`, `shared_preload_libraries=''`, runtime
  metrics listener disabled. AC-07's statement threshold is not verified.
- The collector's relation roots match the pre-change schema. Update its
  canonical roots for F9-T04 and derived roots for F9-T02/F9-T03 before the
  after measurements; the same size accounting must be preserved.
- After measurements at 35 and 365 days, late-data rebuild, retention advance
  and the SPEC-0020/status updates remain pending. F9-T06 is not complete.
