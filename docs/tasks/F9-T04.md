## Outcome and non-goals

Current status, the list sparkline, diagnostics and the consistency check run on
the cumulative/extrema cursors plus independently maintained exact hourly
fallback. Bulk point, tile, repair, display-block and history-job structures are
removed after historical evidence is verified; hourly aggregates, dirty hours,
prefix checkpoints/baselines and pinned-payload byte accounting remain. Workers
wake on due time; only pinned exact-value reads recompute a digest. Keyset pagination
is F9-T05.

## Interfaces and edit scope

- `refreshCurrentProjection`: window, 1 h, 6 h and attainment via `windowTotals`;
  use independent exact hourly fallback when cumulative boundaries are untrusted;
  preserve current-first aggregate admission. No point publication/repair extension
  after the final switch and verified historical-baseline coverage. Sparkline
  per SPEC-0020: rebuilt from
  hourly extrema when the hour closes or when the extrema cursor rebuilt a covered
  hour (compare `published_at`), otherwise only the last bucket is replaced. Stored
  in the projection payload; `budget_history_state` is `ready` unless the covering
  hours are not built, then `preparing`.
- Remove `QueueDueBudgetHistory`, overview history jobs, `AttachPreparedBudgetSparkline`,
  `LatestPreparedBudgetSparkline`, `BudgetPreparationExhausted` and the
  history-admission singleton.
- Digest removal: delete the `sha256(convert_to(payload::text))` predicates from
  `GetPublishedCurrentProjection`, `ListDueDerivedObjectives`,
  `OperationalScopeFingerprint` (unless F9-T05 removes it), `MaterializeSloOverviewReferences`,
  `ListSloOverviewSnapshotItems`, `ListServiceSloStates` and
  `ListSortedCatalogServices`; keep the digest on insert and read of
  `almanac_slo_history_blocks` only. Drop the `payload_digest` columns that lose
  their last reader.
- Due-time wake: each loop computes the earliest `next_attempt_at` or cadence
  boundary from one query and sleeps until it, capped at one second; an idle
  installation issues at most one statement per loop per second.
- Migration `0047_drop_point_pipeline.sql` (provisional; recheck contiguous
  numbering): verify prefix checkpoint/baseline coverage and only then drop bulk
  points, tiles, tile work, series repair, display blocks and history jobs.
  Preserve hourly aggregates, dirty-hour invalidation and their worker/readiness
  queries. Remove legacy series marking only once its last reader has switched.
  Keep the pinned history payload's metadata/digest and byte-accounting trigger
  and table; remove only the display-block accounting branch. Delete obsolete
  code/queries after checking all current/detail/list/diagnostic consumers.
- Diagnostics: stages `aggregate`, `cumulative`, `extrema`, `current`; retry endpoint routes by stage.
- Consistency check and metrics: behind means cursor `built_through` or
  `cumulative_through` older than the last closed hour or the projection stale;
  failing/stopped from cursor attempts and `failing_since`; `derived_bytes`
  sums all remaining derived tables, including fallback, prefix checkpoints,
  bootstrap baselines and their indexes. Cursor lag does not imply every read is
  unavailable; report selected dependencies separately.
- Allowed paths: `internal/storage/**`, `api/openapi.yaml` (stage enum), generated sources, `web/src/slos/client.ts` only if a validator references removed states.

## Acceptance and expected cases

- Current status at a cadence boundary without new observations advances within
  10 s; with a new observation the projection reflects it within 10 s (AC-04 of SPEC-0019).
- AC-03: a late observation changes the current window and affected hours only.
  A 100-day-old arrival cannot block unaffected current 1 h/28 d windows while
  cumulative repair is paused; continuing live observations still publish in 10 s.
- AC-10/11: expired historical prefixes, non-extremum baseline candidates and late
  corrections survive final table removal. Verify both intermediate task states
  retained legacy invalidation and final ingestion still marks the fallback.
- AC-07: on the 20-SLO perf stack after one hour of ingestion, report complete
  `derived_bytes` by table against the identical baseline; demonstrate storage
  reduction with fallback/prefix costs included. Background statements per minute (from `pg_stat_statements`
  or the runtime metrics counter) < 400 while idle.
- Idle installation: with no SLOs, each loop issues at most one statement per second.
- AC-09: stopping work by forcing seven days of failure, then ingesting, restarts it.
- Retention: a partition drop fences/copies exact floors and required prefix checkpoints;
  baseline deltas are folded before their evidence expires. Do not change old
  evaluations to expired merely because their raw inputs expired. Prune prefixes
  by dependency and extrema by endpoint bounds, not bucket-start alone.
- Every e2e journey and browser comparison passes; `make test-perf` at 35 and 365 days.
