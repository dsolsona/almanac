- SLI/SLO lists become usable in <1,000 ms, preferably <=500 ms.
- SLO detail/range/burn actions finish in <1,000 ms, preferably <=500 ms, per the maintainer's 2026-11-01 clarification.
- Exercise 10, 100 and 1,000 SLOs, one year of history and ten concurrent users.
- UI pages default to 20 records, offer 10, and never append unbounded rows.

- Exact counts, extrema, gaps, urgency and immutable table pages remain accurate.
- One unavailable objective cannot block unrelated rows or appear falsely Healthy.
- Preserve live collection, authorization, restart/shutdown and the operator preview.
- Prove rendered journeys and VictoriaTraces; API-only timings and suite counts are insufficient.

### Pagination admission correction (accepted contract amendment)

The live preview with 21 objectives reproduced `resource_limit` on the first-page
SLO list. Its session held four ready snapshots with a maximum payload of only
18,744 bytes. The current OpenAPI contract admits four live snapshots per session
and 64 per installation, including completed results retained for two minutes.
The UI also replaces a preparing first page every two seconds, up to three times.
Changing publication generations makes those replacements distinct snapshots.
Thus the cap measures recent navigation history instead of expensive work and
can reject a fifth ordinary read even when the result is small.

The maintainer accepted the proposed correction with “ok” on 2026-11-01 after
the quota and its replacement were explained. This acceptance covers the OpenAPI
amendment; implementation and runtime acceptance remain separate:

- Remove automatic preparation polling. Keep the confirmed page during an explicit
  refresh, with honest history availability and no hidden job waits.
- Keep limits of four concurrent **preparing** snapshots per actor/session and
  64 per installation; completed snapshots do not consume build slots.
- Account completed snapshots by bytes: 64 MiB per actor/session and 1 GiB per
  installation, preserving the former worst-case bounds (4/64 times 16 MiB).
  Charge at least 16 KiB per completed snapshot to bound small-result metadata.
  Reserve 16 MiB per preparing snapshot under the same byte budgets before work
  starts. Admission remains serialized, bounded and cancellable.
- Retain the existing 16-MiB/10,000-objective per-result limits, two-minute
  nonrenewable expiry, actor/session/role/filter binding and immutable old pages.
  Do not evict valid cursors or increase timeouts to make navigation pass.
- Report resource failures in list terms, without suggesting a nonexistent graph
  range. Preserve existing values and keep actual quota failures visible.

Change scope: source OpenAPI description and generated clients; snapshot-usage
SQL/generated queries and admission logic; overview polling/error copy; focused
unit checks and real PostgreSQL coverage. No dependency, cache service, new queue,
calculation change, auth bypass or operator-volume reset is needed.
