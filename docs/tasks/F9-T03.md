## Outcome and non-goals

Current status no longer publishes points; the list sparkline comes from hourly
extrema rows; diagnostics and the consistency check cover dirty hours, the
extrema cursor and current failures; points, tiles, tile work, repair, display
blocks, history jobs and the admission singleton are gone; workers wake on due
time; only the pinned exact-value payload is digest-checked. Hourly aggregates
and dirty hours stay untouched. Keyset pagination is F9-T05.

## Acceptance and expected cases

- Current status at a cadence boundary without new observations advances within
  10 s; with a new observation the projection reflects it within 10 s.
- Sparkline: identical to the previous build at the same generation; a late
  observation 3 days back changes the affected buckets only after the rebuild.
- AC-07: on the 20-SLO perf stack after one hour of ingestion, `derived_bytes`
  by table against the F9-T06 baseline; background statements per minute per
  SLO below 20 while idle; with no SLOs each loop issues at most one statement
  per second.
- AC-09: forcing seven days of failure then ingesting restarts the cursor.
- Retention: extrema rows ending at or before the cutoff are deleted; a partition
  drop needs no derived preparation.

  remains, current statuses and graphs match except the documented expired
  portion of windows reaching before the raw cutoff.
- Every e2e journey and browser comparison passes; `make test-perf` at 35 and 365 days.
