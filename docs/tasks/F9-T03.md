## Outcome and non-goals

Current status no longer publishes points; the list sparkline comes from hourly
extrema rows; diagnostics and the consistency check cover dirty hours, the
extrema cursor and current failures; points, tiles, tile work, repair, display
blocks, history jobs and the admission singleton are gone; workers wake on due
time; only the pinned exact-value payload is digest-checked. Hourly aggregates
and dirty hours stay untouched. Keyset pagination is F9-T05.

## Interfaces and edit scope

- `refreshCurrentProjection`: unchanged evaluation; delete `publishRetainedCurrent`
  and the repair extension. Sparkline per SPEC-0020: rebuilt from the 168
  covering hourly rows when an hour closes or a covered row's `published_at`
  changed, otherwise only the last bucket is replaced; `budget_history_state`
  from cursor state.
- Remove `QueueDueBudgetHistory`, overview history jobs, `AttachPreparedBudgetSparkline`,
  `LatestPreparedBudgetSparkline`, `BudgetPreparationExhausted`, `LockHistoryAdmission`
  and `prepareHistory`.
- Digest removal: delete `sha256(convert_to(payload::text))` predicates from
  `GetPublishedCurrentProjection`, `ListDueDerivedObjectives`,
  `OperationalScopeFingerprint` (unless F9-T05 removes it),
  `MaterializeSloOverviewReferences`, `ListSloOverviewSnapshotItems`,
  `ListServiceSloStates` and `ListSortedCatalogServices`; keep insertion and
  read of the `almanac_slo_history_blocks` digest. Drop `payload_digest` columns
  that lose their last reader.
- Due-time wake: each loop computes the earliest `next_attempt_at` or cadence
  boundary from one query and sleeps until it, capped at one second.
- Migration `0046_drop_point_pipeline.sql` (provisional): drop
  `almanac_slo_series_points`, `_series_tiles`, `_series_tile_work`,
  `_series_repair`, `_display_blocks`, `_history_jobs`, `_history_admission`;
  reduce `almanac_invalidate_slo_series` to the cursor update; remove the
  display-block branch of the cache-usage trigger; delete their Go and SQL.
- Diagnostics: stages `aggregate`, `extrema`, `current`; retry routes by stage.
- Consistency check and metrics: behind means `built_through` older than the
  last closed hour or the projection stale; failing/stopped from attempts and
  `failing_since`; `derived_bytes` sums the remaining derived tables and indexes.
- Allowed paths: `internal/storage/**`, `api/openapi.yaml` (stage enum),
  generated sources, `web/src/slos/client.ts` only if a validator references
  removed states.

## Acceptance and expected cases

- Current status at a cadence boundary without new observations advances within
  10 s; with a new observation the projection reflects it within 10 s.
- Sparkline: identical to the previous build at the same generation; a late
  observation 3 days back changes the affected buckets only after the rebuild.
- AC-07: on the 20-SLO perf stack after one hour of ingestion, `derived_bytes`
  by table against the F9-T06 baseline; background statements per minute per
  SLO below 20 while idle; with no SLOs each loop issues at most one statement
  per second.
- AC-09: forcing seven days of failure then ingesting restarts the cursor.
- Retention: extrema rows ending at or before the cutoff are deleted; a partition
  drop needs no derived preparation.
- AC-06 migration rehearsal on a copy of the preview database: no old table
  remains, current statuses and graphs match except the documented expired
  portion of windows reaching before the raw cutoff.
- Every e2e journey and browser comparison passes; `make test-perf` at 35 and 365 days.

User acceptance checklist:

- Starting state: seeded stack with the F9-T02 build; SLO list open.
- Expected: sparkline present and updating each minute; detail unchanged;
  diagnostics show the three stages.
- Container journey: `make test-e2e`, `make test-perf`.
