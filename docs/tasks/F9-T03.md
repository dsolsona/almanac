## Outcome and non-goals

The SLO detail endpoint serves graphs, daily compliance and pinned exact-value
pages from complete extrema marks plus live edges and preserved historical
prefixes, with samples identical to the retained path at the same generation,
including the existing receipt watermark. The old path runs until F9-T04 removes it;
current status and the list are unchanged in this task.

## Acceptance and expected cases

- AC-02 equivalence: on the perf seed (20 SLOs, 35 days) and on a 365-day seed,
  for each SLO and each of `7d`, `90d`, `1y`, `window`, with burn 3600 and 21600,
  the samples, `display_coverage`, `retained_extrema` labels, compliance days and
  summary from the extrema reader deep-equal those from the retained reader at
  the same generation, including receipt maxima after out-of-order arrivals.
  Run both readers in one test transaction; independently calculate sparse/gapped
  cases with equal counts but different freshness and observation/receipt times.
- Open hour: a request at 12:07 includes endpoints 12:01 to 12:07 evaluated live;
  the 1y request evaluates at most 7,200 live endpoints and reads at most 400 extrema rows.
- Transitions: an hour with 61 state changes keeps both sides with exact counts
  and temporal evidence; a lane with 1,600 transitions across the range drops
  them and sets `display_coverage`.
- Cadence: 70 s and 7,200 s observations retain their actual canonical endpoints
  across UTC hours/midnight, including hours without endpoints and a compliance
  evaluation before midnight. No synthetic `range_start + n*cadence` grid.
- AC-10: keep a complete 28 d evaluation now 364 days old, expire its raw prefix,
  then accept late data inside the window. Rebuild every candidate endpoint and
  reselect extrema against an independent preserved oracle. Cover checkpoints,
  upgrade baselines, gaps, restart and values not selected as old extrema.
  Advance retention again after the late correction; folding baseline deltas
  before source deletion must preserve values without double-counting on restart.
- Ties: two endpoints with equal attainment keep the earlier one as minimum.
- Late data (AC-03): an observation 3 days back widens `dirty_to` by the window;
  after the worker runs, only hours in `[t, t+W]` have new `published_at`.
- AC-05 crash points for the extrema worker.
- AC-09: a failing cursor shows in `/preparation` with its reason; retry resets it.
