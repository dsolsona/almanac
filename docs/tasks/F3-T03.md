## Reviewed contract

Extend the existing operational detail endpoint and authorized repeatable-read
transaction with compliance days and summary. A row represents a completed UTC
day whose end belongs to `(visible_start, visible_end]`; exclude the unfinished
current day. Request the ordinary rolling-objective evaluation at that day end.
Preserve the existing canonical calculation alignment: disclose the requested UTC
midnight and the effective evaluation boundary at or before it. Do not expand the
general window evaluator to accept otherwise unsupported boundaries.

Each day carries UTC date/start/end, requested and effective evaluation instants,
`slo_id`, definition revision and target, exact quality/performance, and explicit
met/violated/unknown/not-applicable status with explanation. Partial, shortened,
zero-traffic, known-empty, expired and unavailable windows cannot prove met.
Retain observed quantities with provisional qualification where applicable.
Historical completeness determines the historical result; local display and
current live receipt age do not redefine completed-day membership or math.

Summary fields are met, violated, evaluable, unknown and not-applicable counts.
Evaluable equals met plus violated; those classifications partition returned
days. Return at most 365 rows. All four lanes and summary share one fact read and
transaction; later observations can affect only a later response. Current target
semantics are immutable under F3-T01; do not invent semantic revision editing.
Report/export local-date grouping remains a separate contract.

Reuse one cancellable rolling-window sweep over ordered aligned day-end evaluation
instants. Preserve actual-fact limits and minimal source horizons; no per-day SQL
or expansion of absent cadence ticks. Graphs, exact tables and summary consume the
same server objects.

The fourth graph plots actual rolling attainment against the objective target,
including the target when no days are evaluable. Its outcome counts are separate
from the plotted value; a violated day does not become a synthetic zero percent.
