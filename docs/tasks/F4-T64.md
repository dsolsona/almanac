## Acceptance

- An Editor opens the historical backfill disclosure on a Prometheus-compatible
  SLI, reviews the UTC range and chunk count, and confirms the job. A Viewer
  can inspect jobs. A misaligned or out-of-validity range gives an actionable
  error without creating work.
- The assigned agent collects exact historical counts separately from live work.
  Committing an eligible 99/100 interval updates the linked 99.5% SLO to the
  mathematically expected 200% consumption for this synthetic population.
- Refresh, restart, retry and cancellation preserve the durable result. A late
  claim cannot replace a live or committed observation.
- Overdue missing intervals are recovered after the backend and agent reconnect.
  Complete-empty coverage, younger intervals, and exhausted automatic cycles
  are excluded from routine admission.
- The SLI detail retains its existing measurement and collection layout. The
  disclosure and preview are exercised in the isolated browser journey and
  shown in desktop light and mobile dark captures.

  final restart run also exercises the normal deployed agent and backend.
