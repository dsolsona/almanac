## Outcome

Bounded maintenance retains original, latest, last-breaching and transition-pinned
evidence, including active occurrences older than 365 days. Add an explicit
last-breaching evaluation reference; snapshot identity alone does not pin its row.
Prune terminal history only after both effective and detected terminal times are
older than retention. Keep cursor evidence references valid.

Compact only old unpinned contiguous evaluations sharing policy/meaning/exclusion/
calculation revisions, truth and every condition outcome/quality/reason. Preserve
at least 24 hours by both scheduled and committed time. Different source
generations may occur within a compacted range: retain first/last snapshot
identities and explicit unchanged-outcome summary, not invented middle counts.
Compacted numeric history is unavailable. Never compact through a pinned row,
condition handoff, quality/revision change, repeated cutoff, gap or cancellation.
Keep every transition and its exact evidence for the retained occurrence.

All cutoff, clock and batch limits are explicit inputs. Work uses bounded queries
and existing Runtime admission. No cleanup runs against the maintainer's database.

## Acceptance

- Active occurrence older than a year keeps original/latest/last-breaching and
  transition evidence; indeterminate cannot erase the last breach pin.
- Only terminal history older than both clocks is eligible; exact retention
  boundary remains retained. Referenced rows are never orphaned.
- Within-horizon or recently committed catch-up decisions are not compacted.
- Runs cannot cross truth, condition handoff, quality/reason, policy/source meaning,
  mask/calculation identity, holes or pinned rows. Different source generations
  keep bounded first/last provenance and no fake numeric evidence.
- Compaction range insertion and evidence removal commit together or roll back.
  Concurrent maintenance batches cannot duplicate/delete protected work.
- Repeated processing is idempotent; admission/cancellation releases resources.
