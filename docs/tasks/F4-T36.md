## Outcome

Bounded maintenance retains original, latest, last-breaching and transition-pinned
evidence, including active occurrences older than 365 days. Add an explicit
last-breaching evaluation reference; snapshot identity alone does not pin its row.
Prune terminal history only after both effective and detected terminal times are
older than retention. Keep cursor evidence references valid.

Compact only old unpinned contiguous evaluations sharing policy/meaning/exclusion/
calculation revisions, truth and every condition outcome/quality/reason. Preserve
at least 24 hours by both scheduled and committed time. Different source
generations may occur within a compacted range: retain first/last snapshot
identities and explicit unchanged-outcome summary, not invented middle counts.
Compacted numeric history is unavailable. Never compact through a pinned row,
condition handoff, quality/revision change, repeated cutoff, gap or cancellation.
Keep every transition and its exact evidence for the retained occurrence.

All cutoff, clock and batch limits are explicit inputs. Work uses bounded queries
and existing Runtime admission. No cleanup runs against the maintainer's database.

## Acceptance

- Active occurrence older than a year keeps original/latest/last-breaching and
  transition evidence; indeterminate cannot erase the last breach pin.
- Only terminal history older than both clocks is eligible; exact retention
  boundary remains retained. Referenced rows are never orphaned.
- Within-horizon or recently committed catch-up decisions are not compacted.
- Runs cannot cross truth, condition handoff, quality/reason, policy/source meaning,
  mask/calculation identity, holes or pinned rows. Different source generations
  keep bounded first/last provenance and no fake numeric evidence.
- Compaction range insertion and evidence removal commit together or roll back.
  Concurrent maintenance batches cannot duplicate/delete protected work.
- Repeated processing is idempotent; admission/cancellation releases resources.

### Independent review corrections

The review identified two valid issues: overlap absence checks grew with all prior
ranges, and the authenticated range reader discarded the newly persisted summary.
Both are corrected without changing retention ages, core planning, schema or
production registration.

The actual SQLC overlap query is now an indexed predecessor lookup. Cursor-locked
writers advance monotonically and existing ranges for a policy revision are
disjoint; compaction checks before adding a range over already committed
individual evaluations. The greatest start below the proposed end is therefore
the only predecessor that can overlap. The end predicate stays outside `LIMIT 1`.
A real PostgreSQL regression inserts 1,002 disjoint ranges and covers adjacency,
multiple-range overlap, a long gap, administrative cancellation and no predecessor.
Its captured original query plan examined 1,002 rows for an absence proof (red
selection 0.618 s). The corrected actual generated query uses the existing unique
policy/revision/start index backward, examining one predecessor, with no new index
or planner settings. This is a bounded-work proof, not a capacity claim.

`AlertRangeRecord.Summary` now exposes the typed optional `AlertCompactionSummary`
used by both the writer and reader. The reader validates format, condition
outcomes/quality/durations/positive decimal thresholds, aggregate truth, endpoint
identity, nondecreasing source generation and cutoff/range boundaries. Unknown
members, including invented numeric evidence, are rejected. Ordinary ranges have
no summary. Persisted malformed summaries reject the authenticated read without
returning a partial page. The authenticated read-after-compaction regression first
hit the fixture's 12-hour absolute session expiry; issuing a fresh session at the
advanced clock exposed the intended missing-summary failure in 0.412 s. No auth
limit was changed. Missing summary types/validator also fail the initial focused
validation compile as expected.
