`make seed-demo-sli SLI=demo RANGE=7d` selects a single local 99/100
constant event-count SLI with an existing live observation. It fills missing
intervals within the current measurement definition and reports requested and
eligible coverage. It leaves the newest settled interval to live collection.
Observation insertion and derived-data marking share a transaction. Linked SLOs
calculate against their existing validity boundaries.
