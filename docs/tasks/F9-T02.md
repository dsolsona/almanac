## Outcome and non-goals

The SLO detail endpoint serves graphs, daily compliance and pinned exact-value
pages from extrema rows plus live edges, with samples identical to the retained
reader at the same generation. The point, tile and repair pipeline keeps running
until F9-T03 removes it; current status and the list are unchanged here. Hourly
aggregates, dirty hours and the batched window query are not modified.

## Acceptance and expected cases

- AC-01: for the focused fixture and a randomized one (gaps, known-empty, zero
  traffic, fractional and 26-digit counts; cadences 15, 60, 70 and 7,200 s), each
  hourly and daily row's marks equal the reducer's selection over a full
  per-endpoint evaluation, including ties, transitions on both sides, presence
  sets, hours without endpoints and a compliance endpoint before midnight.
- AC-02: on the perf seed (20 SLOs, 35 days) and a 365-day seed, for each SLO
  and each of `7d`, `90d`, `1y`, `window` with burn 3600 and 21600, the samples,
  `display_coverage`, labels, compliance days and summary from the extrema reader
  deep-equal the retained reader at the same generation, in one test transaction,
  including receipt watermarks and freshness after out-of-order arrivals.
- Open hour: a request at 12:07 evaluates endpoints 12:01 to 12:07 live; a 1y
  request reads at most 400 rows and evaluates at most 7,200 live endpoints.
- Transitions: an hour with 61 state changes keeps both sides with exact inputs;
  a lane with 1,600 transitions across the range keeps four marks and sets
  `display_coverage`.
- AC-03: an observation 30 days back widens `dirty_to` by the window; after the
  worker runs, only hours in `[t, t + W + 7 h)` and their days have a new
  `published_at`; a current 28 d window stays usable throughout.
- AC-10: a 28 d window at an endpoint 364 days old rebuilt after late data shows
  the pre-cutoff part as expired and keeps exact retained totals; a neighbouring
  untouched hour is unchanged.
- AC-04 and AC-05 as specified, including an insert committed between claim and
  acknowledgment.

User acceptance checklist:

- Starting state: seeded stack; detail page at 7d.
- Expected: identical graphs and exact tables to the previous build; range and
  burn changes under 1,000 ms, preferably 500 ms; gaps shaded as before.
- Navigation: all four periods and both burn windows; exact-value pagination.
- Container journey: `make test-e2e`; `make test-perf` at 35 and 365 days.
