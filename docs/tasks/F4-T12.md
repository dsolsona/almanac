## Outcome

An operator can inspect why a supplied occurrence is open, its original and latest
exact evidence, and its lifecycle; authorized acknowledgement requests target that
occurrence/version. The component never owns domain decisions or optimistic state.
The catalogue demonstrates actual shared controls/charts with fictional data.

## Acceptance checklist

- One title, real supplied SLO link, severity/lifecycle and eligible acknowledgement.
- Reason, interactive burn evidence, readable calculation and lifecycle cards match
  selected A in both themes at 360/1024/1440 px.
- Matching, not-matching, unavailable and calculation-waiting remain distinct;
  original/latest evidence retains its own exact bounds/time/revisions.
- Hover/touch/keyboard expose exact chart samples and gap reasons without rounding
  a threshold decision or fabricating missing values.
- Pending/error/concurrent resolution, viewer permissions and focus preserve
  navigation and supplied state. Deferred Notes/assignment/close/destinations absent.
- No API, global synchronization, authenticated navigation latency or engine
  acceptance is claimed from the catalogue.
